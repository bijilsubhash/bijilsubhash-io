# Repo conventions

## Typography

- **Never use the em dash (`—`, U+2014) anywhere in the repo** — not in prose, MDX
  content, rendered strings, code comments, or CSS. Rewrite with a colon, commas,
  or parentheses where that reads best; otherwise use a spaced en dash (`–`).
- **Never use the middle dot / interpunct (`·`, U+00B7) anywhere in the repo.**
  For separators (title templates, metadata lines, headlines) use a spaced en
  dash (`–`) instead.
- The en dash (`–`, U+2013) is fine and is the house dash — it's already used for
  date ranges (e.g. `Nov 2025 – Present`) and compound modifiers
  (e.g. `Unity Catalog–enabled`).
