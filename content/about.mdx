I came to data the long way around: a doctorate in chemical engineering, then data science, and finally the engineering work I enjoy most.

## What I do

I am the founder of [NimbleStax](https://nimblestax.com), where I work as a fractional data engineer for early-stage startups and mid-sized organisations building their data platforms from the ground up. The work spans the full stack: ingestion, transformation, orchestration, governance, and infrastructure as code.

Day to day that means dbt, Dagster, dlt, BigQuery, Snowflake, Databricks, Terraform, and Python. Migrating off legacy infrastructure, building config-driven ingestion frameworks, standing up cost-effective architectures on GCP, AWS, and Azure — the throughline is systems that are maintainable, extensible, and built to last.

## How I work

I am a pragmatic engineer. I make decisions that are both functional and cost-effective, and I care about generating a positive return as early as possible. I am a strong advocate for clean, readable code, and I would rather name a tradeoff plainly than hide it behind a framework.
